## Question for Ambrose

**Do MP Materials' commercial agreements give enough revenue visibility to support the recommended financing structure?**

The deck argues that MP could raise equity first and hold an undrawn revolver as a backstop because government and commercial contracts reduced its revenue uncertainty. We want an underwriter's view of that premise:

1. **Visibility before 10X revenue.** Commissioning is expected from 2028. Before then, which contracted amounts are cash, which are accounting income, and which remain conditional or subject to appropriations? Please separate:
   - **recognized PPA income:** $67.962m in 9m 2026, $118.978m for 2025 plus 9m 2026 (our sum of reported amounts, C31);
   - **PPA cash receipts:** $110.9m in 9m 2026;
   - **future offtake entitlements:** threshold and facilitation payments that start only after COD.
2. **Covenant timing (slide 15, trigger 3).** The deck says that if covenants would bind before 10X revenue, equity only is better. Under S09, a covenant trigger replaces the $500m minimum-cash test with leverage ≤4.0x and cash-interest coverage ≥3.0x. Consolidated EBITDA (a)(xiv) allows an addback for Defense-agreement minimum payments, subject to certificate and anti-duplication conditions. Can that addback cover the gap before 10X revenue arrives, or does trigger 3 apply? Our illustration assumes a trigger delivered on 15 August 2027, which makes 30 September 2027 the first earnings-test quarter, 93 to 458 days before the calendar bounds of expected 2028 commissioning (C50). The trigger date is an assumption, not a disclosed event.
3. **Quality of each revenue source.** Please assess four sources:
   - **10X offtake (S03):** a government buyer, a ten-year post-COD term and a threshold EBITDA definition; payments are subject to appropriations (§6.8).
   - **NdPr price floor (S04):** $110/kg through 2035; MP pays 30% of the upside after the milestone.
   - **Apple agreement (S17):** magnets and recycling, with $200m of milestone-related prepayments; it is not a 10X offtake.
   - **Merchant sales:** everything else.

   Which of these would a lender actually underwrite, and on what basis?
4. **Transfer limit.** Slide 15 states that a sponsor without an anchor offtake, a price floor or a fallback preferred may not have MP's equity window. From the commercial side, is that the right list? Is anything missing, such as offtaker credit, tenor, or take-or-pay as opposed to price support?

You may conclude that the revenue evidence supports a different structure. Possible conclusions include equity only; committed debt sized to contracted cash flows; delay until offtake payments start; or a recommendation the deck has not considered. Any of these is acceptable if you show the contractual basis.

## Constraints on the answer

- **Offtake threshold EBITDA is not covenant EBITDA or cash available for debt service.** If you treat it as a debt-service proxy, say so and state the assumption.
- **Threshold and facilitation payments depend on COD (S03 §§6.2–6.3); please confirm the exact start date in §6.2.** Before COD, the contract provides only limited reimbursement, up to $30m a year, of certain incremental pre-COD costs (§§6.4.1, 6.4.5; C23).
- **PPA income and PPA cash differ.** Do not add them together.
- **The Apple prepayments are milestone-related and total $200m.** They are not $500m of immediate cash.
- **No public cost-to-complete exists.** Management expects more than $1.25bn of 10X investment. Quarterly cost-to-complete reports go to DoD under S02 §6.04(c) and are not public. Covenant headroom is not disclosed either. Where your conclusion depends on these, say so; do not estimate them.
- **No forecasts enter the tracker.** Any scenario you build stays in your memo, labelled as an assumption.
