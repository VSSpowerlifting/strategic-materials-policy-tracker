## Selected title and publication argument

**JPMorgan and MP Materials: Financing Through 10X Construction**

JPMorgan helped maintain a financing route across a joint construction commitment, common-equity underwriting and corporate credit. The equity takeout supplied $724.2m of net cash without scheduled repayment, with 7.68% isolated ownership dilution. Government support delivered cash and appears expressly in the corporate credit terms. MP nevertheless retained construction and corporate funding exposure through commissioning. Comparable sponsors and lenders should test replacement financing against dated cash availability, permitted uses and covenant conditions before treating a project as funded through completion.

“Financing through construction” identifies the analytical period and task. It does not certify a fully funded completion budget. The deck must state that boundary locally.

### JPMorgan prominence

JPMorgan belongs in the title as a documented participant across stages and agent on continuing credit. Its advisory role remains explicitly attributed to the bank. Goldman jointly supplied the original commitment and had the same initial equity underwriting allocation as JPMorgan, approximately 40% each. Morgan Stanley underwrote approximately 20% initially and jointly arranged the revolver. Citi and BofA also had roles. The role matrix must appear early, identify the relevant legal entities and avoid interpreting initial underwriting obligations as retained shares or fees.

MP management selected and executed the capital raise, investors supplied equity cash, and government supplied distinct support instruments. Observed bank continuity is a defensible organizing lens. “JPMorgan financed $1bn of construction,” unique bank indispensability, sole design of the covenant, or regulatory-capital relief are unsupported descriptions.
