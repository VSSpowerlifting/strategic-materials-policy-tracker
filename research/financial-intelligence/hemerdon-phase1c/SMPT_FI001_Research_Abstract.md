# Hemerdon Financing and Restart Risk

The National Wealth Fund's Hemerdon transaction provides £61 million of gross equity and core-loan financing within a £71 million headline that includes an uncommitted £10 million accordion. Tungsten West reports a fully funded restart pathway and processing progress. The financing replaces earlier short-term debt and supports redevelopment, while its announced 366-day loan tenor leaves commercial execution and refinancing material. This assessment reconciles the instruments, October remaining-spend guidance and two distinct model sensitivities. An illustrative £5.75 million incremental stress measures cost and rate exposure. Deferring £15 million of expected receipts in the original August plan produces approximately £2 million negative cash on the stated-total convention, or approximately £1 million using rounded detail. Neither result establishes a current deficit. The next investment test is the conversion of commissioning and offtake into collected cash before debt repayment, supported by a reconciled liquidity and debt schedule.
