## Methods and source rights

Instrument figures are gross transaction amounts. Loan drawdown is stronger evidence than an announcement, but neither gross funding nor facility limits measure current cash. Announced tenor origin remains unresolved. The group security deed leaves its lender unnamed; NWF linkage remains inference. Equipment disclosures overlap in scope and are not added.

The October midpoint is a model convention. The cost/rate scenario combines independent drivers without assigning probability or a joint cash-flow date. The August receipt scenario holds original-plan uses fixed and preserves both rounding conventions. It is never added to October guidance or stress.

The deck uses paraphrased facts and original exhibit structures. No AMC tables, proprietary benchmark series, third-party slide graphics, source screenshots or font binaries are distributed. No new rights to source material are asserted. Human editorial approval and source-specific rights review remain release conditions.
