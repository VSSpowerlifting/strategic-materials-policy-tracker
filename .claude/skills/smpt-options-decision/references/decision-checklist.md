# Decision checklist

Mark PASS, FIX or N/A.

- [ ] Decision and decision-maker restated from the mandate.
- [ ] Three to five mutually exclusive options, including the baseline.
- [ ] Criteria tied to the decision-maker's objective, each with defined evidence.
- [ ] Matrix cells categorical, evidence-cited, modeled inputs labelled; no weighted score.
- [ ] Recommendation conditional, attributed as a draft judgment, with its strongest supporting evidence named.
- [ ] At least one trigger that would reverse the recommendation.
- [ ] Competing explanation or option considered and its rejection explained.
- [ ] Monitoring indicators observable from public sources with baselines.
- [ ] No securities advice, price targets or invented policy targets.
