## Why this case is not "conditionally supportable" yet

The canonical claim is well anchored. But it describes an issuer-reported **state**,
"is in commercial production", and the start day is not known. On 11 August 2026 the
Q2 release still described full commercial production as *planned*. The move into
commercial production therefore happened somewhere between 11 August and 14 September
2026, and no source states the day. `occurredOn` must stay null (§7 test 2). The
reviewer must also confirm that "commercial production" is not being read as
"full commercial production".
