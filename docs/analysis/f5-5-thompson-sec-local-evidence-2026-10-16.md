### Date and claim boundary: why this is progress, not automatic permission to publish

1. **Substantially completed construction** (issuer report for Q2) is one reported state. **Partial assets placed in service** is a narrower accounting and operational indicator. **Seven of nine operational** (independent August 27 report) is a later, separately evidenced observation. These are **not** automatically three different public milestones, nor do the sources supply any exact day for physical completion.
2. The June 30 accounting period is not the August 11 filing date, which is not the August 27 news publication date, which is not a known site-visit date. A retrospective press report cannot manufacture a first operation date. The later source is not proof of what was public at the earlier source's clock.
3. A seventh operational furnace does not imply nine furnaces running at once, nor does a large-capacity furnace imply realized tonnage at nameplate. The local article also describes a **separate old seven-furnace smelter**. Do not confuse *seven operational new furnaces* with the *seven old furnaces* in the older building.
4. Thompson Falls **new smelter expansion**, pre-existing smelter, nearby Stibnite Hill mine, and Radersburg flotation facility are distinct pieces of the USAC footprint. Evidence at one does not silently validate operational status or physical completion at the others.
5. All observations are **reported**: an SEC filing is legally filed corporate disclosure; a local field story is journalistic observation and interviews, but neither substitutes for the existing M3 named human reviewer reading the full source and checking the precise facility/scope/date in the original.
