### Wagerup: two publisher organizations report the same ceremony, not two construction starts

- **Alcoa original issuer**, August 24, 2026, [press release](https://news.alcoa.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Australia-Japan-the-United-States-and-Alcoa-Break-Ground-on-Gallium-Project-in-Western-Australia/default.aspx), dateline and first paragraph: the governments and industry partners held a groundbreaking **for the gallium production plant** co-located at Wagerup; Alcoa says this marked its construction start. This is the existing \`src-alcoa-wagerup-groundbreaking-2026\`.
- **Australian Commonwealth, separate institutional source**, August 24, 2026, [Ministers King and Ayres joint release](https://www.minister.industry.gov.au/king/media/ground-broken-landmark-gallium-project), paragraphs 1–3: officials say the first sod was turned on the Alcoa–Sojitz **gallium** project at Wagerup. In [King's Wagerup press-conference transcript](https://www.minister.industry.gov.au/king/media/press-conference-launching-gallium-project), opening statement, the minister describes being on site for the groundbreaking. This supports the occurrence and location of the **ceremony from an additional participant**, not actual progress after it, commissioning or achieved supply.
- **Source-dependence limitation:** company and ministerial releases describe the *same public event* involving the same government partners. They are differently issued, not two independent site-survey reports. An announced expectation that a future operating plant could meet a share of worldwide gallium demand is not realized output. The transcript mentions public finance, but its amount/commitment language must **not** be imported into execution claims or reconciled here with separate signed, paid and equity/offtake instrument records.

**Editorial result:** Alcoa's single \`construction_started\`, \`named_facility\`, \`occurredOn: 2026-08-24\` proposal remains a **ceremony-qualified company-reported** assertion, now independently corroborated as a groundbreaking *event* by a government participant. Three associated finance rows still represent **one proposed project-native milestone**, never three.
