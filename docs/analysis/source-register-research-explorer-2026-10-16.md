# SMPT source-register research explorer — October 16, 2026

The 183 curated sources previously appeared in an unfilterable confidence-tier list. Source dossiers offered direct reverse citations, but researchers could not select original languages, publisher-date unknowns or documents linked to particular published record types.

The /sources page now offers research search by publisher/title/source ID/URL/notes; filters for source hierarchy, document kind, language, publication-day completeness, and explicitly citing record type. Counts and types derive from the **existing canonical source-usage index**, not a second graph. Filter state is shareable via URLs. Full source cards remain in prerendered HTML and links to originals and source dossiers remain intact.

## Limitations

- Directly citing counts represent distinct published records, not field occurrences, independent corroboration, source truth, physical milestones or causal influence.
- A primary original company issuer document is not necessarily government-issued. A citation does not independently verify its claims.
- Unknown publisher day stays unknown; SMPT registered access is a separate observation clock.
- No directly citing record does not mean a source is false or unused elsewhere. Such documents remain filterable.
- No private M3/F5 reviews, capture reports, unpublished sources or source approvals enter this page.
- No seed records, finance statuses, events, milestones, monitors, project review decisions, APIs or exports are changed.

## Verification

Tests reconcile every result to direct citations and reject duplicate or unknown identities. Path-filtered pinned Chromium QA checks static HTML, filters, URL reloads, keyboard navigation, desktop/tablet/mobile and empty states. Full exact-head CI remains a merge requirement. This work is isolated from ongoing F5 original-source research.
