## Admissible evidence

The two DOE selection announcements identify **16 selectees across two funding actions**. DOE's August 20 battery Round 3 programme pages allocate **US$500 million proposed federal share to seven**; its August 18 industrial-feedstock pilot release reports **US$162 million collectively for nine**. These are separate cohorts. The second cohort release supplies no recipient-by-recipient dollar shares. The known combined announced selection value is **US$662 million**, exclusively at the selection/negotiation stage.

The August 18 release explicitly says selections may be rescinded during award negotiations. Both funding actions require subsequent award documentation before claims of a signed grant, a disbursement or completed construction. The source descriptors "awarded" or "investment" in later newsletters are not a substitute for award agreements. Review later original funding disclosure before status promotion.

Individual shares: three at $100m and four at $50m; **$500m total**. Facilities at specific sites have distinct evidentiary footing from broad regions or locations TBD. Nth Cycle named its proposed project **Project SHIELD** and said it would recover nickel and lithium products; cite [the company's own 20 August announcement](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/20/3348717/0/en/u-s-department-of-energy-selects-nth-cycle-to-enter-award-negotiations-for-up-to-100-million-to-build-new-critical-mineral-refining-facility.html) if adding those specific products, and distinguish proposed 24,000-tonne capacity from production.

The DOE sources name ethylene carbonate and silicon-anode products. Their precise materials are outside the current SMPT taxonomy; resist treating their role in lithium-ion batteries as proof they process lithium, graphite or cobalt. A source-based taxonomy proposal can follow the dedicated material phase.

- The **Alcoa USA Corp.** selection is a distinct research case. An existing SMPT Alcoa-Sojitz **Wagerup gallium** project is already registered; the headquarters/corporate connection alone does not identify the byproduct pilot as that project.
