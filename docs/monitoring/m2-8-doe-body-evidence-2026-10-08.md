## M2.8 candidate code — intentionally limited

- `scripts/doe-cmei-body-proof.ts` scopes all evidence to a single original `schema:Article` under DOE's main content and verifies original official title, displayed date and primary office using the previously live-proven M2.7 parser. It extracts **all** publisher `field--name-field-text` + `field--type-text-long` `div` blocks with balanced same-tag matching, as well as publisher `usa-accordion__button` headings. It preserves their DOM order in an **experimental** SHA-256 fingerprint over normalized text **and source-link hrefs**. Site chrome, previous/next navigational rows, unrelated CMS timestamps and footer churn never enter the candidate hash.
- Candidate body limits: max 2MB input HTML, 1–80 field blocks, <=120 accordion headings, text fields 24–80,000 chars each, combined bounded text 170–180,000 chars. Missing official field, unsupported links, malformed nested elements, implausible text, or mismatched publisher article header fail. The output gives *counts*, an experimental candidate digest and a short diagnostic preview. **No full official body text is archived, redistributed or published by this probe**.
- The result explicitly retains `fullBodyBoundaryConfirmed:false` and `eligibleForRevisionTracking:false` even if the candidate fingerprint is stable. The parser may miss DOE **tables, embeds, images/captions, special formatting, headings not in the observed field and accordion types**. Therefore M2.8 is a source-use and completeness gate, not M2.9 production revision-detection authorization.
- `scripts/probe-doe-body.ts` and `.github/workflows/doe-body-proof.yml` are manual-only, read-only, main-scoped, with the same **five bounded DOE HTTP requests** (2 fixed index pages, 3 independent articles) already used in M2.7. Each candidate article must still agree with its title/date/URL/issuer-bound listing record. No EXIM/M1 state or API/policy/finance data is touched.
