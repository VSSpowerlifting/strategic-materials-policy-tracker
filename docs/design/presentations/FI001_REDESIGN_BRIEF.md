# FI001 redesign brief — proposed first application of SMPT v2

**Status:** Editorial design plan only. No alteration to the published FI001 release.  
**Source of truth:** Existing accepted Hemerdon Phases 1A–1C, author-approved final memo, financial model and source register.  
**Target:** **18 slides total = 14 main + 4 appendix**. Absolute ceiling is **20 total**, including any cover, references or appendices.  
**Public title:** *Inside Tungsten West's £71 Million Restart Financing*  
**Subtitle:** *Government Capital, Project Expenditure and Refinancing Risk at Hemerdon*

## Governing analytical question

What did the National Wealth Fund's financing provide for the Hemerdon restart, how do its equity/debt/conditional components differ, and which operating and financial milestones govern the next stage of commercial execution?

The edition should emphasize the independent instrument reconstruction, dated sources-and-uses record, and bounded scenario modeling. Do not create a contemporary cash balance, net present value, debt-service coverage ratio or funding deficit without new verified disclosure.

## The 14-slide main narrative

1. **Cover.** Title, Financial Intelligence 001, Benjamin Yang, issue/revision date and **4 Nov 2026 research cutoff** (unless a separate updated evidence review authorizes a new cutoff).
2. **Financial judgment.** Supported headline: core government-backed financing strengthens restart; short-duration debt creates a repayment/refinancing timetable during ramp-up. Evidence and decision relevance in three clear findings.
3. **Project chronology.** Date the issuer announcements, financing and operating milestones; explicitly separate event and publication dates.
4. **The £71m headline contains only £61m gross core financing.** £36m equity, £25m core loan and £10m uncommitted accordion in a status-safe instrument exhibit.
5. **Equity and core debt allocate financial risk differently.** Ownership, loan obligations, announced tenor/interest and security terms only where supported by accepted source record.
6. **The dated funding plan depends on distinct sources and uses.** Explain August original structure and what later disclosures establish; do not infer current liquidity.
7. **October management guidance leaves £40m–£45m of project expenditure.** Explicitly excludes operating costs and is not the current funding gap.
8. **The illustrative downside raises the modeled project-cost reference by £5.75m.** Quantified components £2.5m + £3m + £0.25m with a note that the rate increment is an analyst proxy.
9. **The August receipt-deferral scenario shows plan sensitivity, not current cash.** Approximate negative £2m aggregates / £1m rounded detail after deferring £15m original expected receipts; keep distinct from slide 8.
10. **Processing and commissioning milestones precede full cash conversion.** Dated reported operations with no claims of independently verified steady-state production.
11. **Commercial offtake establishes a contractual route, while collections remain an observed-data gap.** Elmet agreement, delivery/payment milestones and limits of public evidence.
12. **Short loan tenor links operating ramp to refinancing decisions.** A clear, bounded maturity/operating dependency, no default probability or repayment sufficiency invented.
13. **Stakeholders require different next disclosures.** Investor, lender and public-financier information needs, ranked by impact on the financial interpretation.
14. **Final synthesis.** What the accepted evidence supports, which observations would materially change the assessment, and the next diligence requests.

## Four-slide compact appendix

15. **Source hierarchy and date reconciliation.** Company audited accounts, finance terms, issuer news and NWF releases.
16. **Instrument classification and status register.** Reported equity, core debt, conditional accordion, bridge / equipment finance where relevant.
17. **Model definition and calculation check.** Dated assumptions, scenario baselines, sensitivities and independent verification results.
18. **Open financial information requests.** Current unrestricted cash, current consolidated debt, debt maturity/refinancing arrangements, commercial receipt evidence, operating and cost statements.

No generic "thank you" slide, full-page disclaimer page or three separate empty divider slides. Footnotes and source links belong on the relevant pages. Do not create any slide merely to hit 18.

## Art direction

- Run the [Editorial Authenticity & Visual Integrity Standard](EDITORIAL_AUTHENTICITY_AND_VISUAL_INTEGRITY.md) on final wording, charts, graphics and full-deck coherence, recording all five gate outcomes in the successor edition QC report.
- Standard 16:9 wide.
- Graphite cover/closing, cream analytical pages, gold emphasis.
- Georgia Bold assertion titles; Source Sans 3 supporting text and figure annotations; document fallbacks and export verification.
- Data first: financial instrument/status bars, true arithmetic bridges, scenario decompositions, milestone/conversion sequence.
- Direct labels and readable source footers. If the original model lacks a needed number, disclose that and redesign the exhibit rather than inventing input.
- Editable PowerPoint and PDF; render **all slides** before release. Preserve the existing memo/workbook unchanged unless explicit editorial approval authorizes a substantive amendment.

## Separate release process

1. Build the complete revised deck as a **versioned successor** to FI001's existing PPTX/PDF, never as an unreviewed overwrite.
2. Recheck every numeral against the accepted model and source register; confirm hyperlink and source-rights integrity.
3. Run visual QA of every slide and font-substitution tests.
4. Have the human author review the exact PDF and editorial distinctions.
5. Only after explicit authorization, replace/update publication files and website links via a scoped release PR, verify live URLs, and retain previous versions for reproducibility.

The style-guide documentation itself does **not** authorize this release.
