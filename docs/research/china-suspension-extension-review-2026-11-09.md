## Notes for the data record (no data edited in this task)

1. There is no `src-mofcom-55` and no clause rows for No. 55 of 2025 (superhard materials, DCPCVD equipment and technology). Coverage gap, not an error.
2. The seed rows for No. 61 clauses (end-use, military and advanced-chip review) carry `in_force` from 2025-10-09. The raw text dates only item 1(3) to publication; items 2 to 7 state no separate date. Recommend the maintainer review these status entries.
3. MOFCOM's page metadata describes No. 70 as 商务部公告 while the text is a joint announcement.
4. No. 46 of 2024 paragraph 2 covers superhard materials as well as gallium, germanium, antimony and graphite; No. 72 of 2025 (9 November 2025) suspends the paragraph to 27 November 2026.
5. PubDate values on several MOFCOM pages (for example No. 61, 2025-11-17) reflect re-publication, not issue dates.
